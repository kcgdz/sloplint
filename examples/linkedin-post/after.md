I applied to 47 product manager roles between January and June and got 47 rejections, most of them automated emails within a week. Last month I accepted an offer as a senior PM at a 300-person logistics company.

Two changes made the difference, and neither was about mindset.

First, I stopped sending the same resume everywhere. For the last eight applications I rewrote the top third of my resume to match the job post, and five of those turned into first interviews.

Second, I asked the recruiters who did reply why I was cut. Three answered. All three said the same thing: my case study showed what I shipped but not how I decided what to ship. I rebuilt it around one feature I had argued against and lost, and what the data showed six months later.

If you are in the middle of a search, the case study fix is the one I would make first. It took me a weekend.
