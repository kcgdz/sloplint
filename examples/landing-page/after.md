# Tasks, docs and chat in one app

## Taskflow replaces the three tools your team switches between all day. Teams that move over cut their weekly status meeting from 60 minutes to 15.

Every task has its own doc and its own chat thread, so the decision about a bug sits next to the bug. When someone asks "what's the status?", the answer is already on the card.

[Try it free for 14 days](https://example.com/signup)

## What it does

- **Writes the weekly update for you.** Taskflow drafts a summary from the cards that moved this week. You edit it and send it.
- **Imports from Jira, Trello and Notion** in about five minutes for a team of 50.
- **Shows where work is stuck.** A card that sits in review for more than three days gets flagged on the team board.
- **Keeps your data in the EU or US**, your choice. SOC 2 Type II audited, SSO on every plan.

## Who uses it

About 4,000 teams, most of them between 10 and 200 people. One engineering lead told us their standup now takes six minutes, because everyone reads the board before it starts.
